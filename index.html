DDKit Photo Scanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Purpose

DDKit Photo Scanner is a Chrome extension for authorized DDKit moderators. It helps reviewers initiate a reverse image search for selected profile photos through Google Lens and Yandex. Search is started by a deliberate click on the extension's search control.

Information processed

When a moderator starts a search, the extension accesses the selected image and its source URL on the DDKit administration pages. This may include a photograph of an individual. The extension is not designed to collect passwords, payment details, browsing history, or unrelated content.

How information is shared

For Google Lens, the extension opens a Google Lens search using the selected image URL. For Yandex, the extension retrieves the selected image from its image host and temporarily transfers its image data to the Yandex search page for upload. Both services receive information necessary to perform the requested search and process that information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google and Yandex are independent third-party services. This extension cannot control their data retention or processing practices. Review their policies before using these services.

Storage and retention

The extension temporarily holds image data in browser memory to complete a Yandex search and clears it after completion or tab closure. It does not intentionally store searched photos in extension-managed persistent storage or maintain its own server-side photo database. Search providers may retain or process submitted information according to their own policies.

Use limitations

The extension processes selected images only to carry out the image-search function initiated by an authorized moderator. The extension developer does not sell the images or use them for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

Permissions

Access to admin.ddkit.io is used to display search controls next to photos in the DDKit admin interface. Access to the permitted image hosts is used to retrieve a selected image. Access to yandex.com is used to deliver the selected image to its search interface.

Who should use the extension

This extension is intended only for personnel authorized to work with DDKit profile photographs. Its use and the sharing of profile images with external search services must comply with the organization's applicable policies and permissions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's data handling changes. The latest version will be posted on this page, with an updated date.
